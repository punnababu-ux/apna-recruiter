Introducing

Poneglyph.

apna’s design system. Named after the stone tablets in One Piece that hold the true record: written once, in one script, and read by everyone who comes after.

  1. Written once, read everywhereEvery colour, size and motion is a named token. Change it in one place and every screen follows.
  2. Compose, never re-stylePages are built from existing parts. A new need becomes a new part first, so nothing is drawn twice.
  3. Subtract the obvious, add the meaningfulAfter Maeda’s laws of simplicity: one green action, one italic word per page, and the warmth kept in.
