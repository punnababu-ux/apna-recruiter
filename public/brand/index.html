apna brand guide

How apna looks, everywhere.

One black wordmark, four colours with one job each, and two typefaces. These rules keep apna the same on a phone screen, an ID card and a shopfront.

Draft for review · Pantone and CMYK values still need a printer proof

01 · Wordmark

The wordmark is black, and it stands alone.

The old logo put apna in a box with three stripes. A box makes sense on a phone’s home screen, where every icon must be a square. Everywhere else it fences the name in. So the wordmark now stands on its own, like a signature, and the colours spread across the page as gradients.

Use it as supplied: black on light surfaces and pale gradients, white on black and the dark gradient. Never colour the wordmark. Use the app icon (the one place with a box) only for app stores and small square spaces such as a profile picture.

04 · Colour philosophy

Four colours. One job each.

The old stripes put every colour side by side, so they could only say “apna”. Now each colour has a meaning, used the same way everywhere. People learn it once.

Why these four meanings. Green is the colour people already read as “go”, so it marks the action. Sky is calm and cool, right for help and AI, which should feel quiet, not loud. Gold reads as value, so it marks premium and offers. Plum is the darkest, so it gives the gradients depth.

Mostly calm surfaces. Black carries the wordmark and text. Green appears where we want people to act, and nowhere else. Sky, gold and plum live in the gradients.

The numbers behind the colours

ColourLightness (OKLCH)ContrastWhat that means
Plum38White on plum 10.2:1Darkest. Gives depth to the dark gradient.
Green54White on green 4.7:1Passes WCAG AA for button text.
Sky77Sky on white 2.0:1Too light for text on white. Use it as a fill, or the dark sky (#1B4F8A) for text.
Gold88Black on gold 13.8:1, white on gold 1.4:1Always dark text on gold, never white.

Lightness is how bright a colour looks to the eye (0 black, 100 white). The four sit at different levels, so they stay apart in black and white, on cheap print and on dim screens.

Colour blindness. About 1 in 12 men can’t tell red from green. In a simulation, green and sky get closest. So colour is never the only signal: the main action is a labelled button, AI always has its sparkle icon, and a saving always has its % figure.

Print: colours shift on paper. Match green and the gradient colours to the screen values with a printer proof before a large run.

05 · Gradients

The gradient is the colours meeting.

Soft light, not stripes. The canvas mixes the colours at their palest. A product leans into its own colour. Gradients are always backgrounds: text sits on them or on a white card above them.

The canvas · every page
Jobs · green
AI calling · sky
Unlimited · gold
Database · plum

Do keep them pale enough for dark text, and run one gradient per page.

Don’t use hard colour bands, rainbow sweeps, or a gradient inside the wordmark.

Mix colours the way eyes see them

Sky to gold, mixed the default way (sRGB). The middle goes grey and muddy.

The same two colours mixed in OKLab. The middle stays clean.

Our backgrounds fade each colour into the page rather than into each other. Where two colours do meet, write the gradient in OKLab: linear-gradient(in oklab, …).

Why these two

What we checkedResultWhy it matters
Lowercase heightInter 0.55 of the font size, Arial 0.52, Georgia 0.48Taller lowercase reads bigger at the same size. At 14px, Inter’s is 7.6px against Georgia’s 6.7px.
FiguresInter has tabular figures (all digits the same width)Normally a “1” is about 35% narrower than a “0”. With tabular figures, prices line up and don’t jump when they change.
Headline faceGoogle Sans Flex: one variable file, weights 400–700One download covers every headline weight, and its rounder shapes look clearly different from the Inter text below it.
Indian scriptsNoto SansDesigned to match across scripts, so a Hindi line sits well next to English.

07 · Clay

One warm object, never a crowd.

Our illustrations are soft clay objects in the brand colours. Use one per surface, beside a title or in a corner.

Why clay. It looks made by hand, which suits a company about people’s jobs. An object needs no words, so a key or a flag reads the same in every Indian language. One object per surface keeps attention on the message.

12 · Why these rules

Each rule comes from how people use screens.

Our ruleThe principleWhat it says
One green button per screenVon Restorff effectPeople notice and remember the one thing that looks different.
Few choices at a timeHick’s lawMore options means a longer time to pick one.
The pay button is the biggestFitts’s lawBig targets are faster to hit, and easier with a thumb.
Same thing, same lookGestalt similarityThings that look alike are read as one group.
Taps answer in 150–200msDoherty thresholdReplies under 400ms keep people moving.
Familiar patterns firstJakob’s lawPeople expect our app to work like the apps they already use.
Motion turns off on requestWCAG 2.3.3Some people get dizzy from motion. We follow their phone setting.
